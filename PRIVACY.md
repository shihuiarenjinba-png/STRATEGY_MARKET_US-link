# Privacy Policy — STRATEGY MARKET USA

**Effective date:** October 15, 2026  
**Product:** STRATEGY MARKET USA (Windows desktop business simulation)  
**Support/contact:** [STRATEGY MARKET USA support](SUPPORT.md)

## 1. Scope

This policy covers the offline Windows desktop game STRATEGY MARKET USA. The currently reviewed game build runs locally, without a publisher-hosted account, telemetry, advertising trackers, remote AI/chat features, or cloud synchronization implemented by the game.

Microsoft Store, Windows, GitHub and other separately operated services have their own privacy practices; this policy does not replace theirs.

## 2. Information the game uses and stores

The game processes simulated market information, business decisions, preferences, and save slots **on your device**. Save files may include any names or text you choose to enter. Please avoid entering personal or sensitive information as fictional company or save names.

The game stores its saved state using local application storage provided by Windows/Electron. Users may choose to export or import game data. The game does not automatically send save files to the publisher.

The reviewed build does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or personal account credentials for game play.

## 3. Information sent to the publisher

The reviewed offline game does **not** transmit your gameplay, save slots, identifying information, or analytics to the publisher. No account registration is required to play. The game blocks navigation and application requests to remote web resources in its desktop runtime.

If you contact us voluntarily through the linked public GitHub Issues page, any information you post will be available according to GitHub's settings and policies. Do not post personal information, passwords, product keys, private save data, or other sensitive material in public issues.

## 4. How information is used

Locally stored information is used to continue saved sessions, apply game decisions, and remember settings. Information you voluntarily provide through support is used to investigate and respond to your request.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Your saved game data stays on your device until you delete it using in-game controls (where available) or delete the application's stored files. Uninstalling the software does not necessarily remove all saved user data. Avoid deleting files you still need; keep a backup if you plan to reinstall or migrate.

If you wish to delete a public GitHub issue or comment, use GitHub's available controls or contact us through [support](SUPPORT.md).

## 6. Sharing and third parties

The reviewed game has no publisher-operated cloud sharing or gameplay data collection. Microsoft may independently process purchase, download, license and diagnostic information under Microsoft's policies. GitHub independently handles information submitted via its support service. We do not claim control over those third-party services.

## 7. Security

The desktop build is designed for local-only gameplay and blocks remote navigation. Security measures lower risk but do not guarantee that local files are immune to device loss, malware or operating-system access.

## 8. Children and purchases

The game is not designed to request children's personal information. Purchases and family controls are handled through Microsoft Store and Windows under their own applicable terms.

## 9. Changes and contact

Updates to the game or its data-handling practices may require this policy to change. We will update the effective date and this page when necessary.

For privacy questions, use the [support information](SUPPORT.md).

**Release verification note:** This policy describes the code reviewed on October 15, 2026. Before retail release, the publisher must verify that the final signed/Store MSIX continues to operate as described.
