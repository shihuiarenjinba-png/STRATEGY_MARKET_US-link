# Support — STRATEGY MARKET USA

For questions, installation issues, gameplay bugs and privacy queries:

**[Open a support request (GitHub Issues)](https://github.com/shihuiarenjinba-png/STRATEGY_MARKET_US-link/issues/new)**

GitHub may require you to sign in. GitHub issues are **public**. Do not include your legal name, email, address, product keys, passwords, private save files, or other identifying information.

When reporting a problem, include:
1. Game edition and version (e.g., STRATEGY MARKET USA).
2. Windows 10/11 version and whether you installed it through Microsoft Store.
3. The steps you took and what happened.
4. A screenshot only if it contains no personal or confidential information.

**How to troubleshoot:**
- Launch the game from the Windows Start menu after installation.
- If saving fails, stop further play until your local storage has sufficient available space; do not delete backup save files.
- For upgrade or reinstall issues, back up exported game saves first if the app offers an export command.
- The product is an **offline fictional market simulation**, not a brokerage application, real-time stock-data feed, or financial-advice service.
